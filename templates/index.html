Classroom Telemetry

Multimodal Signals. Deep Analytics.

Temporal engagement timeline evaluated in 30-second intervals combining head pose orientation, landmark movement displacement, inactivity durations, and acoustic voice activity.

Multimodal Fusion Formula (30s Interval)
Engagement = 0.35 × Attention + 0.25 × Movement + 0.20 × (1 − Inactivity) + 0.20 × Audio

Drops are flagged when the 3-interval moving average falls > 20% below the session baseline. Interaction peaks identify collaborative discussions and student responses.

About The Project

🎯

Problem Statement

Traditional roll-call attendance consumes instructional time and is prone to proxy marking. Meanwhile, instructors lack real-time feedback on collective cognitive attention and student fatigue during extended lectures.

🚀

System Objectives

1) Automated biometric attendance with sustained presence verification (≥70% over 60s).
2) Real-time visual and acoustic engagement tracking.
3) Context-aware assistive AI teaching interventions.
4) Absolute privacy with zero raw video or audio persistence.

⚙️

Full Tech Stack

Vision: InsightFace (buffalo_l) ONNX CUDA/CPU + MediaPipe FaceMesh.
Audio: sounddevice + WebRTC VAD + RMS energy.
Backend: Python, Flask-SocketIO, SQLite.
AI Agent: Local Ollama (llama3.1:8b) with heuristic rule fallback.

System Architecture Flow

End-to-end edge pipeline running entirely within the local classroom environment:

01
Hardware & Client Ingestion
Integrated camera (DSHOW / getUserMedia) feeds video and audio chunks over low-latency WebSocket.
→
02
Dual-Stream Feature Extraction
Vision tracks faces, extracts 512-d embeddings and head pose; Audio detects speech activity and RMS loudness.
→
03
Sustained Attendance & Multimodal Fusion
Evaluates 60s rolling presence ≥70% and calculates engagement = 0.35*Att + 0.25*Mov + 0.20*(1-Inact) + 0.20*Aud.
→
04
Edge AI Intervention & Reporting
Flags drops >20%, invokes local Ollama agent, updates live dashboard, and compiles CSV/HTML reports.
→
